A little retro fonduing can make for an evening of romantic or downright silly fun, depending on your companions. Grab a booth and begin with the classic cheese fondue fortified with white wine and kirsch. Then move on to your choice of boiling oil or broth and a themed plate of raw ingredients to cook. The Pacific Rim platter, for instance, arrives with hunks of teriyaki sirloin, peanut-marinated duck breast, chicken, pork-filled pot stickers and shiitake mushrooms, plus two batters to apply before cooking and a handful of dipping sauces for after. For dessert, dip fruit, marshmallows or cake into your favorite chocolate. Settle in for a good gabfest or a round of soul-searching eye contact over the steaming pot, and you'll find yourself plunging into the spirit of the thing.
