Fodor's Review:
Every year when locals vote for Anchorage's best burger joint, Arctic Roadrunner comes out on top. The no-frills decor is mostly 1950s Formica, but if you prefer made-to-order burgers to industrial fast food then this is the place for you. Eat in or drive through at the Arctic Boulevard location and head to nearby Valley of the Moon Park for a sack lunch with the kids. Ultrathick milk shakes and crunchy onion rings are also on everybody's list of favorites. The South Anchorage location has an outdoor deck on Campbell Creek, and in midsummer you can watch spawning salmon swim past.
