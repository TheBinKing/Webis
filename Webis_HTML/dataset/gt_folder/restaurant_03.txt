Set in a cozy cottage festooned with lacy draperies, flickering candles, and a warm hearth, the Grille is the nicest eatery in town. Lunch here is an anything-goes affair, ranging from soups, sandwiches, and large salads to charbroiled burgers and hearty omelets. There are delectable appetizers such as baked brie with apples and pecans, and the "Prime Tickler" (chunks of prime rib served cocktail-style au jus with horseradish sauce). Dinner features grilled and broiled meats, seafood, and prime rib. If it's a nice day, enjoy your meal out on the shady patio.
