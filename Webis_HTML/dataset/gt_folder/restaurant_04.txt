The small, overlit dining room in a half-basement sees few tourists, but I've heard locals brag about how long they were willing to stand in the small entrance area to get a table. When I went, I became a believer, too -- the authentic Thai food is that good and the atmosphere, if lacking in polish, is conducive to a good time. You will need a car, as the location is 20 minutes from town across from the Auke Bay boat harbor. Reservations are not accepted, and they do not have a liquor license.
