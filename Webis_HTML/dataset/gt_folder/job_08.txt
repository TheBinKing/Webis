Job Description:
We are looking for an amazing mechanical engineer to join our team. If you care about building a product that can improve the health of millions and analyzing the world?s single largest repository of vital signs, we want you.

Responsibilities:

* Design a wearable consumer product with emphasis on aesthetic and comfort.
* Design execution of projects from concept to detailed development using Pro-Engineer and/or SolidWorks, including 3D mechanical design and 2D production-level documentation of components and assemblies.
* Engage with contract manufacturer to refine mechanical design, while accounting for manufacturing considerations.
* Perform failure analysis for pre-determining and predicting failure mode and durability of the design.

About You
Requirements

* BS in Mechanical Engineering with 5+ years experience or a MS in Mechanical Engineering with 3+ years experience.
* Thoroughly familiar with 3D CAD and CAE (Pro/E preferred)
* Experience with small plastic/metallic part and fastener design.
* Finite Element experience (statics and dynamics). Vibration and shock experience a plus.
* Experience in prototype processes.
* Experience with the design/development/implementation product cycle.
* Strong communication skills, both written and oral.
* Proven track record of successfully launching products.

Ideally

* Experience with wrist watch design.
* Medical device experience.
* Good understanding of rubber material for wearable devices.
* Ability to translate industrial design into a mechanical design suited for manufacturing.
