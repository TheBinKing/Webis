Einstein Bros brings a stylish, neighborly feel to each of its locations, along with a goodly array of fat, golden bagels. Stop by at breakfast for bagels stuffed with scrambled eggs or make it your lunch stop for an expanded menu of intriguing salads and sandwiches. The Club Mex, with its turkey, smoked bacon, pepperjack cheese, ancho lime mayo and veggies is particularly good.
